## About

**Research Associate in Executive Search** at ZRG Partners, with a background in
**Organizational Psychology** and a specialization in **Business Analytics (Wharton Online)**.

I identify C-Level talent for a living, and I build the tools that make that work faster and
more evidence-based: market mapping, candidate research, pay equity auditing, and AI agents
for sourcing. Most people in HR analytics learn the business afterwards. I started there —
which is why the models I build answer questions someone actually needs answered.

**Currently:** building a relationship-intelligence system on top of my own network —
contact and company graph, cadence rules per relationship tier, RAG over a private corpus,
and an agent that drafts outreach but never sends it. Live front end at
[terryusuchofen.com](https://terryusuchofen.com).

## Selected work

| Project | What it does | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [**Pay Equity Analyzer**](https://github.com/TerryusB/Pay-Equity-Analyzer) | Audits payroll data with statistical rigor: standardizes job architecture (N1–N8), flags retention risk through Compa-Ratios, and tests for gender pay gaps with **OLS regression**. Turns a compensation review from opinion into evidence. | Python · Pandas · Statsmodels |
| [**Headhunting · Mining Executives**](https://github.com/TerryusB/HR-Project-Web-Scraping-for-headhunting) | End-to-end market mapping for operations executives in lithium and copper: X-Ray sourcing, normalization by regex over role, seniority and materials, and a ranked shortlist. | Python · Selenium · Pandas · Excel |
| [**LinkedIn Profile Research**](https://github.com/TerryusB/HeadHunting_Webscraping_linkedin_Profile) | A full selection process for a Marketing Manager role in Peru, filtering 98 candidates down to 66 through structured criteria instead of intuition. | Python · Pandas · openpyxl |
| [**HR Analytics Dashboard**](https://github.com/TerryusB/HR-Analytics-Dashboard) | Attrition analysis: which of promotion, salary, tenure, workload and workplace accidents actually moves the needle on who stays. | Excel · Pivot models |
| [**Hotel Supply · Lambayeque**](https://github.com/TerryusB/Hotel-Analysis-Lambayeque) | Geographic and reputational analysis of hotel supply in northern Peru, from a dataset I built myself out of Google Places. | Power BI |
